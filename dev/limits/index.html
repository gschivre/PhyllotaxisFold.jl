7. Assumptions and limits

Every assumption of the manual is listed below, with the page that introduces it and the signs that it fails. Most can be checked on the data with the package itself: a posterior mass at the edge of the band, a small effective sample size, per-stem terms that disagree with each other.

assumptionwherewhat would break it, and how to check
organs sit on a regular spiral; noise acts on timing, not on positions§2.1a spiral that reverses or changes its angle along the stem: spiral_breaks (Breaks of the spiral); missed organs are not modelled, but a missed organ shows up as a :phase break (Missed organs); a grossly wrong angle is caught as an outlier (§2.4), or appears as a long displacement with outlier = false
von Mises angular noise, one precision per stem§2.2a broad but not uniform error (e.g. angles off by a fraction of the divergence); errors that add up from site to site (sites placed relative to the previous one) may be read as displacements of two or three sites (§6.2)
one twist rate per stem, Normal across stems§2.3a tapering stem makes the rate drift along the stem
displacements bounded by $k = 4$§3.2posterior mass at $\lvert d\rvert = k$; refit with a larger $k$
the deviations of the sites from a regular clock are i.i.d. (independent readiness jitter is absorbed into $f$)§5.2errors that add up (a clock with variable intervals, read as tails beyond about 30 % interval variation, §6.8), cascades of permutations; stems that differ in their off-time rate
the same delay law all along the stem§5.2an off-time rate that changes along the stem (e.g. at the base); a plastochron that changes along the stem, which rescales a delay fixed in hours
the delay law is a Gaussian core with exponential tails§5.6only the readings of the law depend on it (core sd, off-time fraction), not the overtaking profile; check that the fitted law's profile matches overtaking_profile; discrete slot errors (organs exactly one plastochron off) are fitted as short tails; fit another shape and compare its $\Delta\ell$ (Custom delay laws)
(shared-core fit) the groups share one core sd§5.7cores that truly differ: shared_core_test rejects the shared core
the timing law is scored at the propensity model's angle nuisances and band§5.5a small effective sample size of the weights; refit with a larger $k$
flowers open at the pace they were initiated; piecewise-linear opening rate§5.7a maturation delay that changes during flowering; a curved rise or decline

Some questions are beyond the reach of the angles alone, whatever the model:

  • they confound angular noise with permutations (a lower $\kappa$ or more permutations);
  • they identify the delay law only through its overtaking profile, which carries no late/early direction (§5.6);
  • they cannot separate a noisier readiness from a noisier commitment, since only their sum enters the order (§5.2).

Timing data (internodes, imaging of primordia) are needed for those questions.
