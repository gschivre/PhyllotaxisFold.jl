API

PhyllotaxisFold — Module
PhyllotaxisFold

Statistical models of the order of organs along a plant stem, from the absolute azimuths of the organs: the propensity model (free log-propensity of each displacement) decodes the orders, and a law of commitment delays (each site commits after an independent random delay) is fitted through its posterior orders. One module, its source files in dependency order:

filecontent
Permanents.jlthe submodule Permanents: banded permanents (graph, forward / backward, sampling, Viterbi)
Common.jlcanonical angles; log I₀ and Bessel ratio (dual-safe), half-t
Torsion.jlstem twist: coordinates, prior, per-stem rate scan, adaptive quadrature, Newton MAP
AngleLikelihood.jlthe angle likelihood, its analytic gradient and Hessian, the twist integrated out
PropensityPriors.jllog-normal softplus prior on free log-propensities η and its bijectors
PropensityModel.jlthe propensity model PhyllotaxisModelAD: parameters, priors, log density

Decoding.jlper-stem log-likelihoods, corrected angles, organ orders (MAP, posterior, spiral)

The exported names are the interface for an analysis. The building blocks (the banded permanents, the numerical tools, the internals of the likelihood) are not exported; those listed in the manual's API page are declared public (Julia ≥ 1.11) and reached by their qualified names, e.g. PhyllotaxisFold.Permanents.forward!, PhyllotaxisFold.logbesseli. The method is described in the manual (docs/src).

Propensity model

PhyllotaxisFold.PhyllotaxisModelAD — Method
PhyllotaxisModelAD(data, prior = PhyllotaxisPrior(); outlier = true, torsion = nothing, torsion_x = nothing,
    ngrid_ϕ = 32, ngrid_κ = 16, logspace = false)

The propensity model of the stems data (one vector of absolute azimuths per stem, radians, bottom to top); its band k is the length of the prior tuples. Every organ needs a finite angle: NaN, Inf or missing is an error.

  • outlier (on by default): each angle is an outlier (uniform on the circle) with probability π_o, fitted (prior Beta(aπ, bπ)); outlier = false drops π_o.
  • torsion: a TorsionPrior to fit a twist of each stem, ω_s ~ N(m_ω, s_ω²) (degrees per unit of the twist coordinate), each rate integrated out; nothing (the default): no twist. torsion_x: the twist coordinate of each stem (torsion_coordinate(ℓ) from its internodes, in mm, or torsion_coordinate(n), per position, the default). The prior is in degrees per unit of that coordinate.
  • ngrid_ϕ, ngrid_κ: the nuisance grid (offset, precision). Fit coarse grids first, then refine from that solution (fit_map(fine; u0 = coarse_fit.u)). logspace: always run the permanents in log-space (slower; the scaled pass falls back to it by itself when needed).

PhyllotaxisFold.PhyllotaxisPrior — Type
PhyllotaxisPrior(; μΔ, σΔ, mκ = 1.5, sκ = 0.5, νκ = 4, τκ = 0.25, aα = (95,), bα = (5,),
    angles = (Golden_angle, Lucas_angle), aπ = 1, bπ = 49)

Prior of the propensity model; its band k is the length N of the gap tuples (k = 4 by default):

  • η_1..η_k: LogNormalSoftPlus(μΔ, σΔ), gaps with means (0.5, 1.25, 2, 3.75) and variances (0.1, 0.75, 2, 4) by default (η_prior);
  • log μκ ~ N(mκ, sκ²) and σκ ~ half-t(νκ, τκ): the log-normal population of the per-stem κ;
  • the canonical angle of each stem, one of the M + 1 angles (radians), with probabilities set by M stick-breaking probabilities α_j ~ Beta(aα[j], bα[j]): α1 is the probability of the first angle, α2 that of the second among the stems not at the first, and so on; the last angle takes what is left. The default, one Beta(95, 5) and the angles (golden, Lucas), is the golden spiral with probability α ≈ 0.95, else the Lucas one. aα = bα = () with a single angle fixes the canonical angle (no α). List the angles from the most to the least expected: a rare angle is given a Beta that holds its α near 1 at the previous step (e.g. aα = (95, 999), bα = (5, 1) for (golden, Lucas, π)). noble_angle builds angles from noble numbers;
  • π_o ~ Beta(aπ, bπ): the outlier probability (the default Beta(1, 49) has mean 2 %, median 1.4 %), unused with outlier = false.

Numbers are converted to Float64; a single number for aα, bα or angles is a 1-tuple. PhyllotaxisPrior{N}(; ...) also checks the band. The twist prior is given to the model separately (TorsionPrior, keyword torsion).

PhyllotaxisFold.PhyllotaxisFit — Type
PhyllotaxisFit

The result of fit_map: the MAP of a PhyllotaxisModelAD and what is needed to use and check it.

  • model: the fitted model; u: the MAP in the unconstrained space ([model parameters; m_ω; log s_ω] with a twist); θ: the same as a NamedTuple of natural parameters (constrained_parameters).
  • H: Hessian of the log posterior in u at the MAP (-inv(H): the Laplace covariance of u); logposterior, loglik (the log-likelihood, priors excluded, twist rates integrated out) and stem_loglik (its terms, one per stem), gradient (of the log posterior in u at the MAP).
  • converged, iterations, result (the Optim result).
  • twist: nothing without twist; otherwise per stem the most probable rate ω (the centre of its heaviest mode), the posterior mean and sd of the rate (ω_mean, ω_sd, aliases included), the posterior mass of the heaviest mode (mode_mass), and the quadrature nodes.

Use it with decode_orders, order_posterior, spiral_posterior, stem_loglik, corrected_angles, and the StatsBase accessors coef, coefnames, vcov, stderror, coeftable, loglikelihood, dof, nobs. show prints the convergence checks and the parameter table.

PhyllotaxisFold.fit_diagnostics — Method
fit_diagnostics(fit; prior_ratio = 0.9, mode_mass = 0.9)

Checks of a fit_map result, as a NamedTuple:

  • converged, iterations, gradnorm (largest |∂ log posterior / ∂u| at the MAP);
  • hessian_posdef (−H positive definite: the MAP is a strict maximum), hessian_cond (its condition number: large values flag a direction the data barely determine);
  • logposterior, loglik, log_evidence (Laplace approximation of the log marginal likelihood, logposterior + D/2 log 2π − ½ log det(−H), to compare models fitted to the same stems, e.g. with and without outliers or another band k);
  • prior_dominated: the parameters whose posterior sd exceeds prior_ratio × their prior sd (what the data do not inform, so what the prior sets);
  • low_stems: stems whose log-likelihood per organ lies more than 3 robust sd (MAD) below the median (stems the model describes poorly: a measurement problem, another phyllotaxis);
  • with a twist, twist_multimodal: the stems whose heaviest twist mode has a posterior mass below mode_mass (aliases of the twist, integrated over by the fit but worth a look), and twist_at_edge: stems with a mode at the edge of the rate scan (widen nodes_kw = (; scan)).

PhyllotaxisFold.fit_map — Method
fit_map(model; u0, g_tol = 1e-5, iterations = 100, recenter = 1, nodes_kw = (;), options)

MAP of the propensity model by Newton trust region in the unconstrained space, with the analytic gradient and the Hessian from one dual pass over it. Dispatches on the model: with or without outlier angles, with or without a twist (PhyllotaxisModelAD(...; torsion)). Returns a PhyllotaxisFit.

Without twist: one Newton fit from u0 (the prior mean by default). With a twist, each stem's rate is integrated out by adaptive quadrature (nodes on the modes of the stem's own likelihood): 1. fit without twist; 2. nodes at that fit, rates set to each stem's best mode; 3. fit with these rates fixed; 4. nodes at that fit; 5. Newton on the integrated objective in [u; m_ω; log s_ω]; then recenter times: nodes at the new fit and refit. nodes_kw are passed to the node placement (e.g. (; nnodes = 9), nodes per mode; scan). u0 has the model parameters only, or the twist hyperparameters too (a previous fit's u), which then start step 5; by default step 5 starts from the spread of the best modes. The nodes of the fit are stored in the model (its log density is then the objective of the fit).

Convergence: each Newton fit stops when no component of the gradient of the log posterior (in the unconstrained space) exceeds g_tol, or after iterations steps (the fit is then reported as not converged). The default g_tol leaves the parameters off the maximum by about g_tol × their posterior variance, far below their uncertainty on usual data; lower it for data that determine the parameters very precisely. options (an Optim.Options) replaces both, for full control of the optimiser.

newton_map(vgh, v0; options)

Maximise f by Newton trust region, vgh(v, order) returning (f, ∇f or nothing, ∇²f or nothing) up to order. A non-finite evaluation is a rejected step. PhyllotaxisFold.simulate_stems — Method
simulate_stems(η; plants = 100, organs = 50, x = nothing, logμκ = 1.5, σκ = 0.0, α = 0.95,
    angles = (Golden_angle, Lucas_angle), outlier = 0.0, torsion_mean = 0.0, torsion_sd = 0.0, rng)

PhyllotaxisFold.simulate_stems — Method
simulate_stems(fit::PhyllotaxisFit; laplace = false, rng)

PhyllotaxisFold.stem_loglik! — Method
stem_loglik!(fws, grid, y, η, logμκ, σκ, α, with_grad; prune = 1e-14, outlier = nothing)

Log-likelihood of one stem (angles y, already corrected for torsion) given the full log-propensity vector η (2k + 1, η[d + k + 1] = η_d), summed over the nuisance grid (o, ψ, ϕ) × κ nodes, and, with with_grad, its gradient accumulated into fws.grad: [∂ℓ/∂η_d (2k + 1, = E_post[N_d] − E_η[N_d]); ∂ℓ/∂log μκ; ∂ℓ/∂σκ; ∂ℓ/∂α_1..α_M; ∂ℓ/∂π_o]. grid has fields k, ngrid_ϕ, ngrid_κ, ϕ_grid, κ_nodes, κ_logweights and the M + 1 canonical angles; α holds their M stick-breaking probabilities (a tuple). Generic in the number type T of the workspace.

outlier = π_o: each angle is drawn from the von Mises of its site with probability 1 − π_o, and uniformly on the circle otherwise (a gross measurement error, or an organ the site model does not describe). Without it (nothing), the emission is the von Mises alone and ∂ℓ/∂π_o is not written.

Pass 1 runs the forward pass on the whole (ϕ, o, ψ) × κ grid, or only on its points marked in active (a Bool matrix of 4 ngrid_ϕ × ngrid_κ; the others get weight 0). Pass 2 runs forward–backward only on the grid points whose posterior weight exceeds prune (the posterior over the grid is concentrated on a few points), and accumulates the expected displacement counts and expected cosines.

PhyllotaxisFold.decode_orders — Method
decode_orders(fit; full = false, refine = true)

SpiralBreaks

The result of spiral_breaks: stems, one NamedTuple per stem, and the break rate (break_rate: its posterior, summarised, or the fixed value). Per stem:

PhyllotaxisFold.spiral_breaks — Method
spiral_breaks(fit; kind = :orientation, break_rate = Beta(1, 99), min_length = 1)

Breaks of the spiral along each stem of a fit (SpiralBreaks): the posterior number of breaks of each stem, where they are, the log Bayes factor of a break, and the posterior of the break rate across the stems.

  • kind: what changes at a break, besides the phase (each part has its own, uniform): :orientation (the default), :angle (another of the prior's angles), :precision (a new κ from the fitted law), :phase (nothing else), a tuple of them (changing together), or :each (every single kind, a NamedTuple of results).
  • break_rate: the prior probability π_b that a gap between two organs is a break: a number, or a Beta prior shared by all the stems (the default, mean 1 %), integrated out.
  • min_length: the fewest organs in a part.

The population parameters stay at the fit, the parts of a stem share its twist rate, and the order law restarts at each break. Cost: about n/2 stem likelihoods per stem of n organs.

PhyllotaxisFold.GaussianExpTails — Type
GaussianExpTails(σc; πL = 0, mL = 1, πE = 0, mE = 1)
GaussianExpTails(σc, πL, mL, πE, mE)
GaussianExpTails(variant::Symbol; σc = 0.5, πL = 0.05, mL = 1, πE = 0.05, mE = 1)
GaussianExpTails(variant::Symbol, f::GaussianExpTails)

The default delay law, in plastochrons: a Gaussian core of sd σc; a fraction πL of the sites is late by an extra exponential delay of mean mL, a fraction πE early by an exponential advance of mean mE:

f = (1 − π_L − π_E) N(0, σc²) + π_L (N(0, σc²) ∗ Exp(m_L)) + π_E (N(0, σc²) ∗ (−Exp(m_E)))

The parameters share one floating-point type (promoted; ForwardDiff duals inside the fits).

A fit started at this law frees the tails it has, and keeps a tail of mass 0 absent. Free parameters (free_parameters): with both tails, log σc, logit(π_L + π_E), logit(π_L / (π_L + π_E)), log m_L, log m_E (the tail mass and the late share are separate coordinates because the first is better determined than the second); with one tail, log σc, logit π, log m; with none, log σc. The core sd is the parameter shared by the groups of a joint fit, and the one reported by delay_law_inference.

The named variants are starting laws for fit_delay_law, with the tails they fit and nothing else:

varianttailsfree parameters
:corenoneσc
:latelateσc, π_L, m_L
:earlyearlyσc, π_E, m_E
:bothbothσc, π_L, m_L, π_E, m_E

GaussianExpTails(:late) starts from the keyword values; GaussianExpTails(:both, f) starts from the law f (e.g. a fitted one), with the tails of the variant switched on (a tail of mass 0 in f starts at 10⁻⁶) and the others off. :core, :late and :both are nested, so likelihood ratios between their fits test for tails, and on which side.

PhyllotaxisFold.compare_profiles — Method
compare_profiles(a, b; d = 1:3)

Wald test of two independent groups' overtaking profiles (each from overtaking_profile or the profile of delay_law_inference), over the lags d: diff = b − a with its standard errors, χ² with one degree of freedom per informative lag, and its p-value. The default stops at d = 3: an overtaking at distance d needs two displacements that differ by more than d, so with the default band k = 4 the lag 4 rests on displacements near the edge of the band, where the band and the prior of the propensity model weigh most. For groups fitted jointly with a shared core, use the Δprofile of delay_law_inference instead (it accounts for their correlation).

PhyllotaxisFold.delay_law_inference — Method
delay_law_inference(samples, fit; grid)
delay_law_inference(groups, fit; grid)

grid defaults to the grid of the fit (fit.grid).

Robust standard errors at a fit_delay_law result: sandwich covariance H⁻¹ (Σ_s g_s g_sᵀ) H⁻¹ of the unconstrained parameters (per-stem scores g_s, Hessian H of the summed terms, exact by ForwardDiff; clustered by stem, so heterogeneity between stems is allowed), then the delta method. se_model is the model-based (−H⁻¹) version. pinv: a tail with π ≈ 0 leaves its distance unidentified.

estimate and se hold the law's reported_parameters (the core sd σc for GaussianExpTails), the tail masses P(e > 1), P(e < −1), P(|e| > 1), and the overtaking profile q_1 … q_4 (overtaking); names lists them. profile = (; d, q, se, V) repeats the profile with its covariance, for compare_profiles. The profile is identified by the orders; the parameters, the tail masses and the late/early split are readings of the parametric law, identified only through its shape.

For a joint fit of several groups, estimate, se and profile are vectors (one entry per group), and Δprofile and Δoff give each group minus group 1, with standard errors from the joint covariance (the shared parameters correlate the groups); Δprofile also carries its Wald test.

PhyllotaxisFold.fit_delay_law — Function
fit_delay_law(samples, init::DelayLaw = GaussianExpTails(:both); grid, iterations = 200)
fit_delay_law(groups::Vector, init::DelayLaw = GaussianExpTails(:both); grid, iterations = 200)

Delay law maximising relative_loglik, by Newton's method with a trust region (exact gradient and Hessian by ForwardDiff) on its free parameters (free_parameters), starting from the law init, which also sets the family and which parameters are free (any DelayLaw that defines free_parameters and with_free_parameters). Returns (; law, v, init, grid, rel_ll, converged), with v the free parameters of the fitted law and grid the delay grid used (delay_grid(init) by default).

For the default law, the variants name the tails to fit: fit_delay_law(samples, GaussianExpTails(:late)), or GaussianExpTails(:both, f) to start from the values of a law f (see GaussianExpTails).

Given several groups of stems (a vector of order_samples results, e.g. a wild type and its mutant), fits one law per group, with the first nshared_parameters(init) free parameters shared and the others per group. For the default law this is a shared core sd with group-specific tails: the hypothesis that the groups differ in their off-time organs, not in the precision of a typical commitment, and the way to estimate the tails of a group whose own core is not identified (heavy tails). For another law, the shared parameters are those its author declares with nshared_parameters: a parameter with the same meaning in every group (a scale, say). A law that declares none (the default) cannot be fitted jointly, and the call throws an ArgumentError. Returns (; laws, v, init, grid, rel_ll, rel_ll_groups, converged, shared_core = true).

PhyllotaxisFold.loglik_order_exact — Method
loglik_order_exact(σ, logg, z_grid, γ)

Exact log P(σ) for sites 1..n with readiness iγ and i.i.d. delay density g: P(σ) = ∫_{τ_1 < … < τ_n} ∏_p g(τ_p − σ[p] γ) dτ, by a forward recursion on a time grid with step Δz. Two times in the same grid cell are ordered with probability ½ (midpoint rule): counting such ties as ordered would bias log P(σ) upwards at first order in Δz, while the midpoint rule leaves an error of second order. Grid cells where g is more than e^18 below its peak are skipped. g is interpolated linearly between grid points (exact when γ is a multiple of the grid step, as for γ = 1). Generic in the element type of logg (ForwardDiff duals).

PhyllotaxisFold.overtaking — Method
overtaking(f; d = 1:4, grid)

The overtaking profile of a delay law: q_d = P(site i + d commits before site i) = P(e_i − e_{i+d} > d), for each lag in d. With independent delays, q_d is the probability that the organs of any two sites d apart are inverted on the stem, whatever the other sites do: unlike the parameters of f, it is identified by the orders (q_1: neighbour swaps; q_2, q_3: the longer displacements that tails produce). It depends on f only through the law of a difference of two delays, so it carries no late/early asymmetry.

PhyllotaxisFold.overtaking_profile — Method
overtaking_profile(samples; d = 1:4)

The overtaking profile read directly from the posterior orders, with no delay law: q_d is the fraction of pairs of sites d apart whose organs are inverted on the stem, averaged over each stem's posterior samples (order_samples) and pooled over stems (Σ expected inversions / Σ pairs). Under independent delays this estimates overtaking(f) for the true law, whatever f is: a check of the fitted law, and a comparison between groups (compare_profiles) that needs no delay law at all.

The banded permanents:

PhyllotaxisFold.Permanents — Module
Permanents

Banded permanents by dynamic programming over a sliding window of used columns. A permutation σ with |σ[i] − i| ≤ k is a path through a graph whose states are the C(2k, k) masks of used columns in the window {i − k, …, i + k − 1}; each edge assigns row i to column i + d. On this graph:

  • forward!: log of the summed weight of all paths = log perm(W) (log-space, or scaled linear space)
  • backward!: backward messages and the edge marginals P(σ[i] = i + d)
  • sample_permutation!: exact sample (backward filtering, forward sampling)
  • map_permutation: most likely permutation (Viterbi)

Edge log-weights live in ws.w[i, d + k + 1] (in both modes): any position-dependent weight table can be used, toeplitz_weights! fills the stationary case η_d.

Convention: σ[i] is the canonical index of the organ observed at position i, displacement d = σ[i] − i.

source
PhyllotaxisFold.Permanents.PermutationGraph — Type
PermutationGraph(k)

States: masks of 2k bits with exactly k set (bit b ↔ column i − k + b, set = already used). Start = end = (1 << k) − 1. From a mask whose bit 0 is free, row i must take column i − k (d = −k); otherwise it takes any free bit b (d = b − k) or the column entering the window (d = +k). The window then slides by one column (>> 1).

PhyllotaxisFold.Permanents.forward! — Function
forward!(ws, n)

Forward pass on the log-weights stored in ws.w, returns the log-permanent.

  • log-space: F[s, i] = log of the summed weight of all partial paths reaching state s at step i.
  • scaled linear space: row i of the weights is exponentiated relative to its largest entry m_i, the messages are renormalised at each step (Σ_s F[s, i] = 1) and log perm = Σ_i (m_i + log c_i). The row maxima absorb a row where every candidate is unlikely (an outlier angle at large κ); the call is redone in log-space when a step normaliser falls below SCALED_STEP_MIN or the accumulated deficit exceeds SCALED_DEFICIT_MAX (see above), so the result never depends on underflow. ws.inlog[] records which pass produced F.

PhyllotaxisFold.stem_loglik! — Function
stem_loglik!(fws, grid, y, η, logμκ, σκ, α, with_grad; prune = 1e-14, outlier = nothing)

Log-likelihood of one stem (angles y, already corrected for torsion) given the full log-propensity vector η (2k + 1, η[d + k + 1] = η_d), summed over the nuisance grid (o, ψ, ϕ) × κ nodes, and, with with_grad, its gradient accumulated into fws.grad: [∂ℓ/∂η_d (2k + 1, = E_post[N_d] − E_η[N_d]); ∂ℓ/∂log μκ; ∂ℓ/∂σκ; ∂ℓ/∂α_1..α_M; ∂ℓ/∂π_o]. grid has fields k, ngrid_ϕ, ngrid_κ, ϕ_grid, κ_nodes, κ_logweights and the M + 1 canonical angles; α holds their M stick-breaking probabilities (a tuple). Generic in the number type T of the workspace.

outlier = π_o: each angle is drawn from the von Mises of its site with probability 1 − π_o, and uniformly on the circle otherwise (a gross measurement error, or an organ the site model does not describe). Without it (nothing), the emission is the von Mises alone and ∂ℓ/∂π_o is not written.

Pass 1 runs the forward pass on the whole (ϕ, o, ψ) × κ grid, or only on its points marked in active (a Bool matrix of 4 ngrid_ϕ × ngrid_κ; the others get weight 0). Pass 2 runs forward–backward only on the grid points whose posterior weight exceeds prune (the posterior over the grid is concentrated on a few points), and accumulates the expected displacement counts and expected cosines.
